# 🤝 TrustlessPay

**Smart, secure, and automated B2B invoicing and payments powered by AI and blockchain.**

> Bringing trustless efficiency to business transactions — from invoice to payment, without banks or intermediaries.

---

## 🚀 What is TrustlessPay?

**TrustlessPay** is an end-to-end platform for B2B invoicing and payment automation.  
We combine **AI-based invoice parsing**, **smart contract logic**, and **stablecoin payments on the XRP Ledger** to make business transactions fast, auditable, and fully automated.

---

## 🧠 How It Works

1. **Invoice Upload**  
   Users upload invoices (PDF, XML, scanned docs)

2. **AI Parsing & Validation**  
   OCR + NLP extract fields, validate data, detect errors/fraud

3. **Smart Contract Verification**  
   Invoice data is matched against contract terms (amount, delivery, deadline)

4. **Escrow Logic**  
   Funds are held in RLUSD and released only when pre-set conditions are met

5. **Instant Payment**  
   On-chain settlement occurs in seconds on the XRP Ledger

6. **Audit & Notifications**  
   Every action is recorded and visualized via dashboard + alerts

---

## 💡 Why Blockchain?

- **Escrow smart contracts** remove the need for trust or manual approval  
- **RLUSD stablecoin** ensures instant and low-fee settlement  
- **XRP Ledger** enables 3–5s transaction time and full transparency  
- All actions are traceable, immutable, and regulator-ready

---

## 🔧 Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: React + Tailwind  
- **Backend**: Node.js + Express  
- **Database**: MongoDB (Atlas)  
- **AI**: Custom Python pipeline (OCR + NLP + validation rules)  
- **Blockchain**: XRP Ledger + RLUSD (Ripple stablecoin)  
- **Smart Contracts**: Escrow logic via Hooks or multi-sig
